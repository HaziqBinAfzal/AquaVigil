Analysis Method

  1. Parse and validate the supplied evidence without sending traffic or commands to operational systems.
  2. Evaluate water/process values against documented safe demonstration ranges.
  3. Build a recent evidence baseline and identify statistically unusual movement.
  4. Correlate authorization, zones, connection state, signatures, and physical-process context.
  5. Prioritize findings by potential public-health and operational consequence.
  6. Calculate bounded risk, optimization, and asset-health indicators with visible explanations.
