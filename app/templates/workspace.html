{% elif section=='integrations' %}
ZEEK

Network visibility and behavior

Role: Zeek converts observed network traffic into structured connection evidence. AquaVigil reads exported records; it does not scan or contact OT devices.

What AquaVigil evaluates

  • Source and destination endpoints
  • Protocol and connection state
  • Source/destination zones
  • Cross-zone authorization

How to read the result: denied states show rejected/reset sessions; cross-zone values show zone boundaries traversed; an unauthorized cross-zone record becomes a segmentation finding.

Analyze Zeek sampleDownload CSV
SURICATA

Signature-based alert evidence

Role: Suricata produces EVE alerts when traffic matches a configured rule or signature. AquaVigil preserves the alert context for investigation.

What AquaVigil evaluates

  • Signature and severity
  • Source and destination endpoints
  • Network/application protocol
  • Relationship to other process evidence

How to read the result: a signature is investigative evidence, not automatic proof of compromise. Operators validate it against Zeek, asset, and water-quality context.
